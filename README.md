# RemindMe

RemindMe is a free reminder app for iPhone and iPad. Type or say one sentence, for example "remind me to call mom tomorrow at 8 pm", and it creates a reminder with the right date, time and repeat. It works with Siri, can ring a real alarm on iOS 26 and later, reminds you at places, and keeps everything on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

It understands Turkish, English, French, German and Arabic.
